# REC prior-art verification checkpoint — 2026-09-02

Status: targeted web verification, not a systematic review.

The REC shadow-world framing must not claim priority for imperfect detection or sequential sensor failures.

Verified nearest neighbours include:

- Findlay, Briers & White (2020), *Mammal Research*, DOI `10.1007/s13364-020-00478-y`: camera-trap detection decomposed into pass, trigger, image registration and sufficient image quality, with independent CCTV used to quantify false negatives and condition dependence.
- Jones et al. (2011), *Ecology*, DOI `10.1890/10-2403.1`: ecological threshold inference can be distorted when imperfect detection/observation-process variation is ignored.
- the broader occupancy/imperfect-detection literature: nondetection is not absence and ecological inference can be biased by heterogeneous detection.

Consequences for REC positioning:

1. do not claim that missed events or condition-dependent detection are new;
2. do not claim the first independent-reference audit of camera trigger failures;
3. locate the contribution in the auditable chain from an independently generated exposure universe through acquisition, gate evaluability, gate result and record entry, before later TNOA semantic coarsening;
4. require ecological-consequence evidence and cross-system validation before strengthening novelty wording.
