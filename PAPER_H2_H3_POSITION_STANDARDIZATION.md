# H2→H3 position-standardization robustness test

Status: **pre-result robustness specification for the pooled-composition confounding question.**

## Reviewer question

The pooled Findlay results show that wet otters are underrepresented after trigger and badgers are overrepresented after trigger/capture. Because pass composition differs among physical CT positions, a pooled change could in principle be amplified or generated by changes in the position mixture rather than within-position record-entry selection.

## Frozen test

Use the same commit-pinned Findlay tables already used in the paper.

For each ecological endpoint, compare the reference composition and recorded composition **within each physical CT position**, then standardize both worlds using the same position weights.

No row-level p-value will be used because repeated passes are not independent biological individuals and the otter system contains only two captive animals. The evidence target is robustness of the estimand shift to position stratification, not a pseudo-replicated significance test.

## Endpoint A — otter wet/dry

For each camera model/setting separately (`A`, `BS`, `BV`):

1. at each CT position, calculate truth wet proportion among all wet/dry passes;
2. at the same position, calculate wet proportion among confirmed triggered records;
3. record the within-position shift `recorded - truth`;
4. report the number of evaluable positions with negative shift;
5. standardize truth and triggered composition over the same positions using:
   - equal position weights;
   - pooled pass-count weights fixed from the reference pass world.

Also report within-position wet and dry trigger probabilities and their difference where both states occur.

Robust support for the H2→H3 chain requires the standardized triggered wet composition to remain below standardized truth composition in all three camera model/settings. Position-level directions may be heterogeneous and will be reported unchanged.

## Endpoint B — fox/badger species

For the Bushnell-video wild system:

1. at each CT position, calculate truth badger proportion among CCTV-confirmed passes;
2. calculate badger proportion among triggers at the same position;
3. calculate badger proportion among confirmed final captures at the same position;
4. report within-position shifts;
5. standardize truth/trigger/capture composition using equal-position and reference-pass weights.

Robust support requires standardized badger composition to remain above standardized truth composition at trigger and final capture under both weighting schemes.

## Missing-state rule

- unresolved trigger states are excluded only from the stage-specific recorded composition denominator and counted explicitly;
- a confirmed non-trigger is final non-entry;
- capture unresolved after a confirmed trigger remains unresolved and is not recoded as capture failure;
- stage-specific unresolved counts are reported.

## Interpretation

Passing this robustness test would not establish a causal biological effect or independent-position replication. It would establish a narrower but important point:

> **The observed composition distortion is not merely a consequence of pooling different physical CT positions; it remains after reference and recorded worlds are compared under the same position distribution.**

Failure is retained and would require narrowing the H3 claim to pooled composition only.
